# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + Tailwind CSS (SPA, static deploy ready), with GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll, Three.js / React Three Fiber, and Framer Motion.

## Users

Luxury & bespoke concierge travellers seeking tailor-made heritage, wellness, and experiential domestic stays across India. They evaluate agencies on editorial taste, seamless high-touch itinerary curation, verified boutique stays, and direct concierge responsiveness.

## Product Purpose

Priti Travels Hub is an Indian domestic tours and bespoke travel agency showcasing curated regional journeys across India with a travel-film editorial experience ("Discover Incredible India, One Journey at a Time."). Success means visitors feel the richness of each Indian destination immediately and convert into bespoke trip enquiries via the Quick Search Bar, Enquiry Modal, or direct WhatsApp / Call concierge touchpoints.

## Positioning

Instead of generic brochure grids, Priti Travels Hub frames domestic Indian travel as cinema-grade, concierge-tailored journeys—combining boutique heritage & wellness stays, interactive regional exploration (3D India map/globe), 24x7 concierge support, custom itineraries, verified hotels, and a best-price guarantee.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on desktop and mobile while dreaming of or planning multi-day domestic getaways (Rajasthan, Kerala, Himachal Pradesh, Goa, Uttarakhand, Ladakh, Kashmir, Sikkim, Andaman). Primary conversion flows are exploring destinations/packages, searching by destination/dates/travellers/budget, and submitting an enquiry or opening WhatsApp concierge.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed front-end template deliverables: all 17 ordered sections (Preloader, Hero with interactive state cards + autoplay + Ken Burns + parallax, overlapping Quick Search Bar, Popular Destinations, Interactive 3D India Globe/Map by region, Featured Tour Packages with 3D tilt, Experiences by theme, Scroll-pinned 4-step How It Works, Why Choose Priti Travels Hub counters & trust points, Special Offers countdown banner, Photo/Video Gallery, Draggable Testimonials slider, 3 Blog/Travel Guides cards, Instagram-style Reel Strip, CTA Banner, Newsletter Signup, Footer + sticky WhatsApp/Enquiry + back-to-top).
- Enquiry modal with client-side validation (name, phone, destination, dates, travellers, message).
- Centralized editable JSON/config data for all destinations, packages, testimonials, guides, reels, and offers.
- Accessibility & resilience: `prefers-reduced-motion` support and static fallback if WebGL fails.
- Performance target: 90+ Lighthouse with lazy-loading and optimized imagery.
- Note: Business contact details, package prices, testimonials, and seasonal offer countdowns are realistic placeholder content stored in editable config files pending live agency assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- No pre-existing assets or repository files in workspace.
- Realistic placeholder copy and verified Unsplash imagery of real Indian destinations (Udaipur City Palace, Kerala backwaters, Manali snow, Goa beaches, Ladakh Pangong lake, Jaipur Hawa Mahal, Rishikesh, Darjeeling/Sikkim, Andaman) will be stored in structured config files for easy swapping.

## Product Principles

1. **Cinema Before Brochure:** Lead with full-bleed motion, atmospheric typography, and regional storytelling before commercial grids.
2. **Concierge Clarity:** Make "Plan My Trip", Quick Search, Enquiry Modal, and WhatsApp concierge effortlessly reachable without breaking editorial immersion.
3. **Config-First Content:** Every destination, package, testimonial, offer, and image URL lives in a clean external data layer for instant customization.
4. **Graceful Resilience:** Pair WebGL, GSAP ScrollTrigger, and Lenis motion with WebGL fallbacks and `prefers-reduced-motion` respect.
